# Know Your Personality

Welcome to **Know Your Personality**! 🎉

This interactive program allows users to learn more about their personality based on some simple questions. It generates a **secret personality code** and provides a fun overview of your favorite things and spirit animal.

## Features
- **Name & Age**: Get personalized insights based on your name and age.
- **Favorite Things**: Share your favorite food, color, and hobbies.
- **Spirit Animal**: Discover your spirit animal based on your input.
- **Personality Tribes**: Based on your age, you will be classified into one of three personality tribes:
  - **Young Explorer** (Under 18)
  - **Adventurer** (Between 18 and 30)
  - **Wise Owl** (Above 30)

## How It Works
1. **User Input**: The program asks a series of questions such as your name, age, favorite food, color, and spirit animal.
2. **Personality Code**: It generates a unique personality code using the first letter of your name, the last digit of your age, the first letter of your spirit animal, and the first letter of your favorite color.
3. **Personality Tribe**: Based on your age, it assigns you to one of the personality tribes: `Young Explorer`, `Adventurer`, or `Wise Owl`.
